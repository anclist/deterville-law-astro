The definition of who is Canadian just got broader as the Government of Canada has decided not to appeal the decision that strikes down the first-generation limit to Canadian citizenship by descent.

Like many in the profession, I await clarification on the definition of “substantial connection to Canada” coming out of [Bjorkquist et al. v. Attorney General of Canada, 2023 ONSC 7152](https://www.canlii.org/en/on/onsc/doc/2023/2023onsc7152/2023onsc7152.html).
