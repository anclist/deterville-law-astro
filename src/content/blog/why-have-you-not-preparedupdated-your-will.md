Many people do not prepare a Last Will and Testament for several reasons:

1. **Procrastination.** People often delay creating a Will, thinking they have plenty of time or that it’s not an immediate concern.
2. **Fear of death.** Discussing one’s mortality can be uncomfortable and even frightening, leading to avoidance.
3. **Complexity.** Some believe making a Will is a complex and costly process, which can be a barrier to getting started.
4. **Lack of awareness.** Many people are not fully aware of the importance of a Will and how it can benefit their loved ones.
5. **Belief in informal arrangements.** Some rely on verbal agreements, assuming their wishes will be honoured without a legal document.

Creating a Will with the help of a trusted legal professional can make things better in several ways:

1. **Asset distribution.** A Will lets you specify how your assets and property should be distributed, increasing the chances your wishes are followed.
2. **Guardianship.** You can name a guardian for your minor children, ensuring their well-being if you’re not there to care for them.
3. **Avoiding intestacy laws.** Without a Will, the province’s intestacy rules decide how your assets are distributed, which may not match your wishes.
4. **Minimizing family conflict.** A clear Will reduces disputes among family members by setting out your intentions.
5. **Tax efficiency.** A well-structured Will can potentially reduce estate taxes, preserving more for your beneficiaries.
6. **Peace of mind.** Knowing you’ve taken steps to secure your family’s future.
